## 1. What ads are top education advertisers running (US and Mexico/LatAm), preferring long-running ones?

### Takeaway
I catalogued 30 entries:
- 16 concrete ads or ad families on Meta or social (E01, E03–E07, E09–E11, E13, E14, E16, E19, E21, E24, E25).
- 8 account-level library profiles (E02, E08, E12, E15, E17, E18, E22, E23).
- 1 non-Meta stunt (E20).
- 5 Mexico/LatAm context entries (E26–E30).

**What they show:** high-volume education advertisers lean on cheap, text-forward statics, not polished brand video.
- **Offer-first banners** are the most common pattern: Domestika 34% of its library, DataCamp and University of Michigan 22% each, EWA's 90% "only for today", Babbel's "BIGGEST SALE", Platzi's limited-time catalogue price.
- **Universities** (UTEL in Mexico, University of Michigan in the US) lead with split-screen, headline and listicle statics built on a flexibility message.
- **MasterClass is the outlier:** cinematic instructor trailers that end on a catalogue-and-CTA end card.

**Limits:**
- No source gave run durations, so "long-running" could not be verified for any ad.
- Mexico-specific evidence is thin: UTEL is the only Mexican university with a library-level view.
- I found no ads at all for UNITEC, UVM, Insurgentes, Tecmilenio, Tec, Anáhuac, UNIR, Quick Learning, Berlitz, EBAC, Henry, Laboratoria, "prepa en un examen" or licenciatura-ejecutiva advertisers.

#### 3b. [SÍNTESIS] Wireframe cards ("maquetas") — rebuild instructions
**Default canvas for every card:**
- Make a 4:5 master (1440×1800) for Feed and a 9:16 version (1080×1920) for Stories and Reels.
- In 9:16, keep all text, badges, logo and CTA inside the central safe band: y≈270–1250 px, x≈65–1015 px. Specs come from the sibling note.

Zone percentages below are top-to-bottom shares of canvas height. Spanish copy lines are **templates, not observed copy**; the observed wording each one derives from is listed under "Evidence wording".

**A1 · Offer-first banner ("Beca/Descuento primero")** — derived from E24, E15, E08, E17, E16, E22, E23, E18
- **4:5 grid:**
  - 0–8%: logo bar, logo top-left.
  - 8–40%: OFFER BLOCK. One giant number ("50%", "-90%") or a struck-through old price next to the new one, plus a label word ("BECA", "DESCUENTO" or the event name).
  - 40–75%: visual (student cut-out or program image).
  - 75–88%: program name plus one line with the condition or deadline.
  - 88–100%: CTA pill.
- **9:16:** same stack, starting the offer below the top 14% and ending the CTA above the bottom 35%.
- **Hierarchy:** offer > program > deadline > CTA > logo.
- **Evidence wording:** "Hot Days" (UTEL), "BIGGEST SALE" / "New Year's SALE" (Babbel), "only for today" (EWA), "$49.99 → $0.99" (Domestika), "CODER WEEK" / "FLASH SALE" / "MITAD DE AÑO" (Coderhouse site).
- **Copy formula (template):** "[EVENTO] · [OFERTA NUMÉRICA] en [programa/catálogo] · Solo hasta [fecha] · [CTA]". Mexico variant: "Beca de hasta [X]% en tu [licenciatura ejecutiva] · Inicio [mes] · Solicita informes".
- **Caveat:** "Beca X%" was not observed. Its conditions must be visible (sibling-note PROFECO background).

**A2 · Split screen** — derived from E08 (30%), E22 (24%), E23 (13%) and Motion's definition
- **4:5:** vertical 50/50 split.
  - Left: photo or video loop of the target learner, e.g., a working adult at a laptop for licenciatura ejecutiva.
  - Right: solid brand-colour panel with logo (top 10%), H1 (2–3 lines), 2–3 check bullets, one badge chip and a CTA pill at the bottom.
- **9:16 (my adaptation):** horizontal split, visual in the top ~45% and text panel below, all inside the safe band.
- **Variants:** before/after ("Hoy: [situación]" | "Con tu título: [resultado]"); "Presencial vs. En línea"; program A vs. B.
- **Evidence wording:** UTEL's proposition is flexibility (paraphrase).
- **Copy formula (template):**
  - Left caption: "[Identidad/situación]", e.g., "Trabajas de 9 a 6".
  - Right: "[Programa] 100% en línea · [beneficio de flexibilidad] · [CTA]".

**A3 · Headline / text-first static** — derived from E22 (17%), E08 (14%), E15 (13%), E06 (bold colours, simple layout, logo)
- **Layout:** 1:1 or 4:5, solid or gradient brand background.
  - H1 fills 40–50% of the area, 6–10 words.
  - One subhead line.
  - Optional sticker badge top-right.
  - Logo bottom-left, small CTA bottom-right.
- **Copy formula (template):** "¿[Pregunta de identidad o dolor]?" or "[Afirmación de resultado]", then "[Programa] · [modalidad]", then the CTA.

**A4 · Listicle / benefit stack** — derived from E22 (16%), E15 (22%), E17 (13%), E03 (cost → flexibility → small classes → accelerated-program CTA)
- **4:5:**
  - H1 "[N] razones para…" in the top 18%.
  - 3–5 rows, each an icon plus no more than 6 words, from 18% to 80%.
  - CTA and logo in the bottom 20%.
  - Optional photo cut-out on the right third.
- **Evidence order (E03):** cost first, then flexibility, then class size.
- **Copy formula (template):** "[N] razones para estudiar [programa] en [marca]: [costo/beca] · [flexibilidad] · [acompañamiento/grupos pequeños] · [duración/inicio] · [validez]".

**A5 · Student or graduate face hero, with start date and proof** — derived from E01 (graduate with diploma, flexibility, accreditation, "Terms Start Every 2 Months") and E07 (smiling nursing student, "Take the Quiz")
- **4:5:**
  - Full-bleed photo covering at least 60% of the area, face in the upper-middle third, eye contact.
  - Bottom 30% gradient band with H1 (program plus main benefit), a badge row (accreditation; Mexico: RVOE) and a start-date line.
  - CTA pill; logo top-left.
- **Evidence wording:** "Terms Start Every 2 Months"; "Take the Quiz".
- **Copy formula (template):** "[Programa] [modalidad] · [beneficio] · Inicio cada [periodo] / Próximo inicio: [fecha] · [CTA]".
- **Not observed:** "Inscripciones abiertas" is a candidate line only.

**A6 / A7 · Overlay devices**
- **Urgency (A6):** a chip top-right or a thin band above the CTA. Evidence: "only for today", "Terms Start Every 2 Months", "limited-time".
- **Trust badge (A7):** a chip next to the program name, never in the bottom 35% of 9:16. Evidence: only SNHU's "accreditation".
- **Mexico template:** "Validez oficial SEP · RVOE [número]". Unverified as an observed ad element, but the prepa-fraud context (E29) argues for showing it.

**A8 · Quiz, level test or program match, with an Instant Form** — derived from E07, E21, E28*
- **4:5:**
  - Question H1 in the top 25%.
  - 3–4 answer "chips" (A/B/C) in the middle, styled as buttons.
  - Student face on one side.
  - CTA button "Hacer el test" (evidence: "Take the Quiz").
- **Form:** 3–7 questions, staying under the advised 8 (E21), ending in a program recommendation (the "match").
- **Copy formula (template):** "¿[Pregunta de autoidentificación]? Descubre [tu nivel de inglés / tu carrera ideal / tu ruta tech] en [N] preguntas". The "Conoce tu nivel" angle for language schools is unobserved but suggested by Open English's free leveling exam.

**A9 · Instructor / expert / spokesperson** — derived from E09, E11, E12, E14, E23, E24. Video version in section 4.
- **Static cover:** instructor portrait on the left 60%, with a lower-third carrying name and credential. Course or program title as H1. Catalogue claim plus CTA in the bottom band.
- **End-card pattern (E09):** black background, catalogue claim, logo, CTA.
- **Copy formula (template):** "[Nombre], [credencial], te enseña [habilidad]", then the end card "[N] cursos/programas · Acceso ilimitado / 100% en línea · [CTA]".

**A10 · Testimonial, UGC or influencer** — derived from E13 (customer POV, certificate, 7-day trial), E14 (illustrator UGC, 30-day trial), E18, E23 (former-student testimonials)
- **Static quote card:**
  - Photo on the top 40% or left half.
  - Quote of no more than 3 lines in large type.
  - Name, program, current job or result.
  - Offer chip and CTA.
- **Video:** see section 4.
- **Copy formula (template):** "Yo [situación previa] y en [tiempo] [resultado] con [marca]", plus "[prueba gratis / beca]".

**A11 · Platform demo / screen recording** — derived from E17 (30%; "10–15 minutes a day"), E19 (tap-to-translate), E23 (9%)
- **Layout:** phone or laptop mock-up centred at 55–60% of the height, with the screen recording or a screenshot inside. H1 above, two feature callouts with leader lines, offer and CTA below.
- **Copy formula (template):** "Así se ve aprender [X] en [N] min al día" plus "[reto de 28/30 días / oferta]".

**A12 · Carousel of programs** — derived from E10
- **Cards:** 3–6 cards at 1:1 or 4:5.
  - Card 1: hook ("Elige tu programa").
  - Middle cards: program photo plus name, mode/duration and one benefit.
  - Last card: one unifying offer (E10's "one subscription serves two very different interests") plus CTA.
- **Copy formula (template), last card:** "Una sola [inscripción/beca] para [todo el catálogo/cualquier área]".

**A15 · Comparison / us-vs-them** — derived from E18 ("anti-Duolingo") and E26* (Coderhouse vs. Henry: format, support, payment)
- **Layout:** two-column table with the brand's column highlighted and 4–6 rows of ✓/✗. CTA below.
- **Copy formula (template):** "[Marca] vs. [alternativa genérica, e.g., 'cursos tradicionales']: [horario] · [acompañamiento] · [pago] · [validez]".
- **Caveat:** naming competitors is risky under Mexican comparative-advertising rules. That is background, not verified.

**A16 · Campus or landmark hero with format chips** — derived from E04
- **Layout:** landmark photo covering 70%. A chip row: "Escolarizada · Ejecutiva · En línea · Fines de semana". Logo and CTA.

**A17 · Parent-directed** — derived from E05
- **Layout:** parent-and-teen photo. H1 is a question to the parent; 2–3 support bullets (admissions, scholarships); question-style CTA.
- **Copy formula (template):** "¿Tu hijo/a está por entrar a [nivel]? Te acompañamos en [admisión/becas]".

**A18 / A19 / A20**
- **A18 brand character:** top-of-funnel only. Evidence: Crehana's looping heart and Duolingo's Duo.
- **A19 promo event or referral:** event-name lockup plus offer plus date, or "Recomienda y gana hasta $[monto]". Evidence: UTEL's referral up to 2,000 MXN, "Hot Days", Coderhouse event names, #InglésAl100.
- **A20 talent-led:** depends on budget. Evidence: MasterClass celebrities and Open English's humour personalities.

#### 3c. [SÍNTESIS] What to tell designers
- **Start with three static layouts:** A1 offer-first, A2 split screen and A4 listicle. They are the shapes that dominate the libraries of the university and edtech advertisers with format data (E08, E15, E22, E23, E24).
- **Add Mexico-specific layers as variants**, not new layouts: the A7 trust badge, the A6 start date and A19 events or referrals.
- **Video:** use A9 instructor and A10 testimonial for courses, bootcamps and languages; use A5 student hero for universities, licenciaturas ejecutivas and prepa.
- **Prepa (Acuerdo 286 / examen único):** avoid fraud-like speed promises without official backing (E29). Pair any speed claim, such as "en 4 meses", with the official route and the RVOE or SEP information. This is my inference from the fraud reporting.

### Gaps
- I found no ad showing "Validez oficial SEP", RVOE, "Beca X%", "Inscripciones abiertas", a countdown timer, a career-outcome stat card, or a webinar or free-class invite from a Mexican education brand. These need direct capture from the Ad Library.
- I found no K-12 private-school ads, US or Mexican.
- The counts rest on a small, convenience-sampled set of 30 entries, many of them account-level. They show recurrence, not performance.
- Motion's format percentages come from AI tagging, and formats can overlap.
- No canvas, ratio or text-position data was reported for any single ad. All wireframes are reconstructions.
- I did not verify whether Mexican law requires the RVOE in school advertising; the sibling note flags this as background only.

## 4. What video ad patterns do these advertisers use (hook, beats, length, on-screen text)?

### Takeaway
The evidence splits into two poles:
- **Premium cinematic instructor trailers (MasterClass):**
  - Length 15–60 s (unverified).
  - A celebrity or expert's story and worldview, with emotional beats and no syllabus.
  - Varied cuts to hold attention.
  - A black end card with a catalogue claim, logo and CTA.
- **Low-fi performance video (Platzi, EWA, Jumpspeak, Skillshare, Coursera, UTEL):**
  - Spokespeople and former-student testimonials.
  - UGC-style creator endorsements.
  - Screen recordings of the product.
  - Split-screen video with text.
  - Each pairs a trial or discount with urgency.

**Hooks:** the vendor rule of thumb is to win in the first 2–3 s.

**No evidence found:**
- Open English "level" hooks.
- Babbel UGC (Babbel's observed ads are sale-led).
- Duolingo Meta ads. Its paid role is mainly reactivation, plus stunts such as a 5-second Super Bowl spot synced with a push notification.

**[SÍNTESIS] Video beat sheets for Superleads.** All use a 9:16 master with burned-in Spanish captions and on-screen text inside the safe band. Timings are starting points to test, not measured findings.

**V1 · "Tráiler de instructor" (from MasterClass E09/E11/E12, Domestika and Platzi), 30–45 s.** Fits cursos, diplomados and bootcamps.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|

| 3–20 s | Story and worldview, with varied cuts (class B-roll, hands, whiteboard, students). |
| 20–27 s | Lead-gen adaptation, not MasterClass practice: one card with mode, duration and start date. |

**V2 · "Beneficios en secuencia" (from Jamestown E03), 15–20 s.** Fits uni online, licenciatura ejecutiva and prepa.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Cost or scholarship hook. Price claims must follow PROFECO total-price rules (sibling background). |
| 3–8 s | Flexibility ("estudia a tu ritmo"). |
| 8–12 s | Support or class size. |
| 12–15 s | Fast-track CTA ("termina más rápido" / next start). |
