## 1. Legal and advertising requirements for private education in Mexico

### Takeaway
**The advertising legend.**
- The rule sits in Ley General de Educación (LGE, 2019) **Art. 148**, not Art. 149.
- Every ad by a private school with authorization or RVOE must carry a legend with six items:
  - incorporated status;
  - number of the agreement;
  - date of the agreement;
  - modality;
  - the address it was granted for;
  - the issuing authority.
- Art. 150 requires providers of studies without RVOE to say so in their advertising.

**Fees and promotions.**
- The 1992 SECOFI-SEP "bases mínimas" agreement (for elementary and secondary-level schools) requires costs in writing before enrollment.
- The LFPC requires promotion conditions and their duration in the ad itself (Art. 48), plus the total price (Art. 7 Bis, covered in the sibling notes).
- The Supreme Court (SCJN) confirmed in June 2026 that PROFECO can resolve families' complaints against private schools.

**Other points.**
- "Beca" ads are commercial promotions. The legal scholarship duty (at least 5% of students per program, Art. 149) is a separate obligation.
- 2026 enforcement against schools without RVOE is active: the SEP and PROFECO closed the "Harvard" school in Puebla, and Coahuila and Yucatán issued alerts.

### Cited Findings

#### Ley General de Educación (2019; last reform DOF 15 Jan 2026)
- **Art. 148 (legend in documents and advertising), verbatim:** "Los particulares que impartan estudios con autorización o con reconocimiento deberán mencionar en la documentación que expidan y en la publicidad que hagan, una Leyenda que indique su calidad de incorporados, el número y fecha del acuerdo respectivo, modalidad en que se imparte, domicilio para el cual se otorgó, así como la autoridad que lo emitió." — [mley.mx, LGE art. 148 (undated)](https://mley.mx/LGE/articulo/148/); [SDV compendium, art. 148, text current to 15 Jan 2026](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-general-de-educacion/articulo-148/); [Justia México, LGE Título Décimo Primero, cap. I (undated)](https://mexico.justia.com/federales/leyes/ley-general-de-educacion/titulo-decimo-primero/capitulo-i)
  - The brief's reference to "Art. 149" is a misattribution: the legend is in Art. 148, as all three compendia agree.
- **Art. 149 (obligations of private schools with authorization or RVOE):**
  - Comply with constitutional Art. 3 and the law.
  - Follow the current study plans and keep them updated.
  - Grant scholarships: "Otorgar becas que cubran la impartición del servicio educativo, las cuales no podrán ser inferiores al cinco por ciento del total de alumnos inscritos en cada plan y programa de estudios."
  - Sources: [mley.mx, art. 149](https://mley.mx/LGE/articulo/149/); [SDV, art. 149](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-general-de-educacion/articulo-149/)
- **Art. 150 (studies without RVOE), verbatim:** "Los particulares que presten servicios por los que se impartan estudios sin reconocimiento de validez oficial, deberán mencionarlo en su correspondiente documentación y publicidad." — [SDV, art. 150, text current to 15 Jan 2026](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-general-de-educacion/articulo-150/); [Justia México](https://mexico.justia.com/federales/leyes/ley-general-de-educacion/titulo-decimo-primero/capitulo-i)
  - **Earlier history:**
    - Under the 1993 law the equivalent rule was Art. 59. — [H. Congreso de Nuevo León press note (Oct 2019)](https://www.hcnl.gob.mx/sala_de_prensa/2019/10/tipificaran_como_delito_imparticion_ilicita_de_educacion.php)
    - A 2016 federal initiative proposed removing the disclosure provision; the search summary did not show its rationale. The 2019 law keeps the duty, now as Art. 150. — [Cámara de Diputados comparative table (2016)](https://sitllxiii.diputados.gob.mx/cuadros_comparativos/2PO1/0772-2PO1-16.pdf)
  - **Stricter state rule (example):**
    - A Nuevo León legislative note says the state law prohibits legends about pending procedures (such as "RVOE en trámite").
    - It argues that the general law only requires disclosure when there is no RVOE, and that this gap enables deception.
    - The Congress also moved to make "impartición ilícita de educación" a crime; I did not verify whether that was enacted.
    - Source: [H. Congreso de Nuevo León (Oct 2019)](https://www.hcnl.gob.mx/sala_de_prensa/2019/10/tipificaran_como_delito_imparticion_ilicita_de_educacion.php)
- **Current status of the law:**
  - The consolidated LGE shows its last reform in the DOF on 15 January 2026, from two decrees:
    - an omnibus gender-equality decree;
    - a decree on inclusive education for Indigenous and Afro-Mexican communities, migrants and farmworkers.
  - Nothing found suggests they changed Arts. 148-150; the SDV compendium shows those articles' text as current to that date.
  - Sources: [Cámara de Diputados, LGE reform index](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/ref/lge.htm); [DOF 15 Jan 2026 (PDF)](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/dof/2026/ene/DOF_15ene26.pdf)

#### Higher education: LGES (2021) and SEP Acuerdo 17/11/17
- **Ley General de Educación Superior (LGES) Art. 71:**
  - The RVOE is granted for a specific study plan at a specific address (*domicilio*).
  - When a state authority grants it, the private school may teach only in that state.
  - The authority must answer an application within 60 business days, extendable by 30.
  - Sources: [mley.mx, LGES art. 71](https://mley.mx/LGES_200421/articulo/71/); [SDV, LGES art. 71 (no reforms since 20 Apr 2021)](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-general-de-educacion-superior/articulo-71/)
- **SEP Acuerdo 17/11/17:**
  - Published in the DOF on 13 November 2017; in force since 9 February 2018; it replaced (abrogated) Acuerdo 279.
  - It sets the requirements and procedures for RVOE in higher education ("tipo superior, en todos sus niveles y modalidades"), general guidelines for school operation, and inspection and supervision of private institutions.
  - Sources: [SEP DGAIR, Acuerdo 17/11/17 (PDF)](https://dgair.sep.gob.mx/storage/recursos/DREOE/acuerdo_171117.pdf); [SIDOF, DOF note 5504348](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5504348); [UAT book chapter (undated)](https://libros.uat.edu.mx/index.php/librosuat/catalog/download/171/143/428?inline=1)
  - **Amendments:**
    - Acuerdo 08/04/20 changed the application windows during the pandemic.
    - Acuerdo 02/02/24 (DOF 28 Feb 2024) amended it again.
    - Sources: [SEP Tlaxcala copy of 02/02/24 (28 Feb 2024)](https://transparencia.septlaxcala.gob.mx/unidades/despacho_secretario_de_educacion_publica_del_estado/departamento_de_asuntos_juridicos/2024/172.%20A020224M171117TPRVETS.%2028-feb-2024.pdf); [SEP Oaxaca copy (Apr 2025)](https://www.oaxaca.gob.mx/sep/wp-content/uploads/sites/97/2025/04/Acuerdo-numero-02022024-por-el-que-se-establecen-los-tamites-y-procedimientos-relacionados-con-el-reconocimiento-de-validez-oficial.pdf)
  - **Not retrieved:** the agreement's specific advertising article or articles. Three targeted searches failed; see Gaps.
- **Bachillerato:**
  - SEP Acuerdo 450 (2008) sets the RVOE requirements for private upper-secondary (bachillerato) schools.
  - It gives the SEP inspection and supervision powers over private schools, whether or not they are incorporated.
  - Source: [SEP DGB copy of Acuerdo 450 (2023 upload)](https://dgb.sep.gob.mx/storage/recursos/2023/07/H6bPSMihJE-acuerdo450.pdf)
- **Job training (*formación para el trabajo*):** Mexico City's science and education ministry (SECTEI) publishes RVOE guidelines for this category, so some vocational and short courses can carry an RVOE. — [SECTEI CDMX, Lineamiento 6 (2023)](https://www.sectei.cdmx.gob.mx/storage/app/media/RVOE_2023/Lineamiento-6-Formacion-trabajo.pdf)

#### The 1992 SECOFI-SEP agreement on information for private educational services (PROFECO enforces it)
- **Name and issuers:** "Acuerdo que establece las bases mínimas de información para la comercialización de los servicios educativos que prestan los particulares". It was published in the DOF on 10 March 1992 and issued jointly by the then commerce ministry (SECOFI) and the SEP. — [Yucatán state copy of the DOF text](https://yucatan.gob.mx/docs/regulaciones/92ee0c70.pdf); [El Siglo de Torreón (2020)](https://www.elsiglodetorreon.com.mx/noticia/2020/pide-profeco-conciliar-pago-entre-colegios-y-padres-de-familia.html)
- **Scope:** private providers with authorization or RVOE offering "educación elemental y media" (per the DOF text). El Siglo de Torreón describes this as basic and upper-secondary schools. — Same sources.
- **Art. 2 (written information before enrollment, every school cycle).** Providers must give parents, guardians or users in writing:
  - the agreement's own content;
  - the list of educational types and grades offered;
  - the date and number of the incorporation agreement, or else the school's registration number as a non-incorporated school and a statement that its studies lack official validity;
  - (fr. III) the total cost of enrollment or re-enrollment and of tuition, including the number of tuition payments.
  - Sources: [Yucatán copy of the DOF text](https://yucatan.gob.mx/docs/regulaciones/92ee0c70.pdf); [Gobierno de México FAQ on private schools (Apr 2024)](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/906795/Preguntas_relacionadas_con_la_prestacion_de_servicios_educativos_impartidos_por_escuelas_particulares.pdf)
- **What can be charged:**
  - Only enrollment or re-enrollment, tuition and, where applicable, incorporation fees can be charged generally and compulsorily.
  - Schools cannot force families to buy uniforms, supplies or new books from specific suppliers.
  - Sources: [Expansión (25 Aug 2023)](https://expansion.mx/economia/2023/08/25/cuotas-escolares-y-otros-conceptos-escuelas-privdadas); [Gobierno de México back-to-school guide 2023 (PDF)](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/844376/GUIA_DE_REGRESO_A_CLASES_2023.pdf)
- **Other rules:**
  - Tuition paid in the first 10 calendar days of the month must be accepted without surcharge.
  - A family that gives two months' notice before the cycle starts gets back what it paid.
  - Three or more unpaid tuition payments release the school from its obligation.
  - Changes to fee items must be announced at least 60 days before re-enrollment.
  - Sources: [Gobierno de México FAQ (Apr 2024)](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/906795/Preguntas_relacionadas_con_la_prestacion_de_servicios_educativos_impartidos_por_escuelas_particulares.pdf); [El Siglo de Torreón (2020)](https://www.elsiglodetorreon.com.mx/noticia/2020/pide-profeco-conciliar-pago-entre-colegios-y-padres-de-familia.html)
- **Possible update:** Legislative comparative tables from 2024-2025 cite a proposed decree ordering the SEP and PROFECO to update the 1992 agreement within 60 days. I found no evidence it passed. — [Cámara de Diputados table 1334 (2025)](https://sitl.diputados.gob.mx/LXVI_leg/cuadros_comparativos/1PO2/1334-1PO2-25.pdf); [table 0211 (2024)](https://sitl.diputados.gob.mx/LXVI_leg/cuadros_comparativos/1PO1/0211-1PO1-24.pdf)
- **PROFECO guidance, 2025 school season:**
  - Costs must be disclosed in advance.
  - Tuition cannot rise mid-cycle without express approval from a majority of parents.
  - Extraordinary fees need majority approval.
  - Forced purchases are improper.
  - Schools cannot withhold certificates or documents over debts.
  - Complaints line: 800 468 8722.
  - Sources: [El Imparcial (26 Jul 2025)](https://www.elimparcial.com/dinero/2025/07/26/escuelas-privadas-no-pueden-subir-colegiaturas-sin-aprobacion-de-padres-profeco-aclara-derechos/); [El Imparcial (3 Aug 2025)](https://www.elimparcial.com/dinero/2025/08/03/esto-debes-hacer-si-ya-no-puedes-pagar-la-colegiatura-de-tu-hijo-segun-la-profeco/); [UnoTV (undated)](https://www.unotv.com/nacional/colegiaturas-uniformes-y-utiles-lo-que-si-y-no-pueden-exigir-las-escuelas-privadas/); [Criterio Hidalgo (undated)](https://criteriohidalgo.com/noticias/mexico/profeco-advierte-colegios-no-pueden-retener-documentos-por-adeudos); [IDC (24 Aug 2026)](https://idconline.mx/corporativo/2026/08/24/derechos-y-obligaciones-al-elegir-una-escuela-privada-profeco)
- **Hardship (recommendation only):** The federal FAQ urges schools to offer families in payment difficulty some support scheme; this is a recommendation, not a legal duty. It also says the SIGED system can be used to check whether a school has RVOE. — [Gobierno de México FAQ (Apr 2024)](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/906795/Preguntas_relacionadas_con_la_prestacion_de_servicios_educativos_impartidos_por_escuelas_particulares.pdf)
- **Enforcement example:** In Querétaro (2025) a private school was closed (*clausurada*) after imposing a mandatory charge that is not allowed. — [N+ Querétaro (2025)](https://www.nmas.com.mx/queretaro/sociedad/educacion/profeco-advierte-cobros-indebidos-escuela-particular-queretaro-2025-clausuran-institucion/)
- **PROFECO's jurisdiction confirmed (June 2026):**
  - The SCJN confirmed that PROFECO can intervene in disputes between families and private schools.
  - Families can claim for improper charges, breach of contract, or conditions different from those offered.
  - That last point is directly relevant when a school changes an advertised scholarship or promotion.
  - Sources: [Expansión (24 Jun 2026)](https://politica.expansion.mx/mexico/2026/06/24/scjn-profeco-resolver-quejas-escuelas-privadas); [Expansión (25 Jun 2026)](https://politica.expansion.mx/mexico/2026/06/25/profeco-derechos-padres-alumnos-escuelas-privadas)
- **Complaint volume:**
  - January 2020 to February 2023: 3,984 complaints against private schools.
  - Higher education was the largest group, with 1,403.
  - Most concerned improper fee collection; 123 schools were sanctioned.
  - Source: [Expansión (21 Mar 2023)](https://politica.expansion.mx/mexico/2023/03/21/profeco-recibe-3-984-quejas-contra-escuelas-privadas-en-3-anos-y-sanciona-a-123)
- **PROFECO's Buró Comercial:** families can use it to see complaints per provider. They should also search the school's legal name, which may differ from its brand. — [Expansión (12 Aug 2026)](https://expansion.mx/finanzas-personales/2026/08/12/como-revisar-quejas-escuela-privada-profeco)

#### Promotions under the LFPC: becas, "inscripción $0", Buen Fin offers
- **Art. 46** defines what counts as a promotion. — [IDC guide (23 Feb 2024)](https://idconline.mx/corporativo/2024/02/23/guia-completa-sobre-ofertas-y-promociones-proteja-sus-derechos-como-consumidor)
- **Art. 48:**
  - The ads must state the promotion's conditions and its duration or volume.
  - If no term or volume is set, the promotion is presumed indefinite until it is publicly revoked through the same media.
  - Any consumer who meets the conditions is entitled to the offer during the term, or while supply lasts.
  - Sources: [mley.mx, LFPC art. 48](https://mley.mx/LFPC/articulo/48/); [SDV, LFPC art. 48 (no reforms after 23 Dec 2025)](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-federal-de-proteccion-al-consumidor/articulo-48/); [IDC guide (23 Feb 2024)](https://idconline.mx/corporativo/2024/02/23/guia-completa-sobre-ofertas-y-promociones-proteja-sus-derechos-como-consumidor)
- **Not found:** a PROFECO rule written specifically for scholarship ads. A 2019 report found that university financing "becas" (loans) lack a specific regulatory framework, and that CONDUSEF supervises only financial institutions, not schools. — [El CEO (2019, date per search summary)](https://elceo.com/politica/alumnos-con-prestamos-o-creditos-educativos-en-universidades-viven-en-un-limbo-legal/)

#### "Titulación automática"
- **Meaning:** the study plan already includes, in its final cuatrimestres (four-month terms), the work or evaluation that replaces the thesis. The graduate gets the title on completing the credits, with no extra professional exam.
- **Validity:** what makes the title valid is the program's own RVOE, not whether there is a thesis.
- **Caveat:** these sources are student-recruitment blogs and commercial pages.
- Source: [Mextudia (undated)](https://mextudia.com/titulacion-automatica-sin-tesis-en-arquitectura/)

#### Data privacy for leads (LFPDPPP 2025) and minors
- **The new law:** published in the DOF on 20 March 2025, in force 21 March 2025, repealing the 2010 law. — [Greenberg Traurig (Mar 2025)](https://www.gtlaw.com/es/insights/2025/3/nueva-ley-general-proteccion-de-datos); [EY Law Flash (2025)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/es-mx/technical/tax/documents/ey-law-flash-nueva-lfpdppp.pdf)

  - Tacit consent remains valid where the law does not require express consent, and there are more exceptions where no consent is needed. — [IDC (28 Mar 2025)](https://idconline.mx/corporativo/2025/03/28/lfpdppp-5-cambios-clave-en-el-manejo-de-datos-personales)
- **Minors (unconfirmed for the 2025 text):**
  - A compendium page for the LFPDPPP art. 8 reproduces: "Cuando se trate de datos personales de menores de edad o personas en estado de interdicción, se requerirá el consentimiento de quien ejerza la patria potestad o tutela, según corresponda." — [SDV, LFPDPPP art. 8 (version uncertain)](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-proteccion-datos-personales/articulo-8/)
  - The search summary could not establish whether this is the 2025 text, so check it in the official PDF. — [Cámara de Diputados, LFPDPPP (PDF)](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf)
- **Children's rights law (LGDNNA):**
  - Art. 76 recognizes children's right to privacy and to protection of their personal data.
  - Art. 77 treats any direct handling of a minor's image, name, personal data or identifying references in the media as a privacy violation when it harms their honor or reputation, contradicts their rights or puts them at risk.
  - Art. 78 requires written (or other) consent from whoever holds parental authority or guardianship, plus the child's own opinion, for interviews.
  - The text is current to 15 Jan 2026.
  - Sources: [SDV, LGDNNA art. 77](https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-general-de-los-derechos-de-ninas-ninos-y-adolescentes/articulo-77/); [mley.mx, LGDNNA art. 77](https://mley.mx/LGDNNA/articulo/77/)
  - Pending proposals would extend Art. 77 to digital media (2023) and address "sharenting" (2025). They have not been confirmed as passed. — [Cámara de Diputados bulletin (2023)](https://comunicacion.diputados.gob.mx/sintesis/LINKSIN/13223_P_BOLETINES_0_PROPONENCONSIDERRAR_VIOLACIONINTIMIDADMENORES_3DIP.pdf); [Gaceta Parlamentaria (7 Oct 2025)](https://gaceta.diputados.gob.mx/Gaceta/66/2025/oct/20251007-II-2.html)

Parents are the normal contact point for K-12 enrollment. — [Fox Sports MX (12 Jan 2026)](https://www.foxsports.com.mx/2026/01/12/sep-preinscripciones-20262027-de-preescolar-primaria-y-secundaria-en-linea-fechas-y-calendario/)

### Inferences
These are drafts for counsel review, not legal advice.

**Legend template under LGE Art. 148.** Put it in every ad that names a program or school.
- Higher education or bachillerato with RVOE:
  > "Estudios con Reconocimiento de Validez Oficial de Estudios (RVOE) otorgado por [SEP / Secretaría de Educación de (estado)], Acuerdo No. [____] de fecha [dd/mm/aaaa], modalidad [escolarizada / no escolarizada / mixta], para el domicilio [calle, número, colonia, municipio, estado]."
- K-12 schools with authorization:
  > "Incorporado a [SEP / autoridad estatal]. Acuerdo de autorización No. [____] de fecha [____], modalidad escolarizada, domicilio [____]."
- Studies without RVOE (Art. 150):
  > "Estudios sin reconocimiento de validez oficial."
- The law says "en la publicidad que hagan", which plausibly includes Meta ads. Repeat the legend in four places:
  - the creative's footer or the primary text;
  - the instant form intro or context card;
  - the landing page;
  - the first WhatsApp message.

**Multi-program ads.**
- A carousel of 20 licenciaturas cannot hold 20 full legends.
- The common workaround is a short legend plus a link to an RVOE page listing every agreement. That is a legal risk under a strict reading of Art. 148.
- Safer options: one program per ad or card, each with its full legend; or a full legend per card.

**Never write "RVOE en trámite" or "validez oficial" before it is granted.**
- Nuevo León's state law bans legends about pending procedures.
- RVOE is granted per plan and per address (LGES Art. 71).
- A brand-level RVOE claim does not cover a new online program.

**Geotargeting.**
- RVOE granted by a state limits teaching to that state (LGES Art. 71).
- Geotarget in-person programs with state RVOE to that state.
- For online programs with state RVOE, have counsel confirm whether national targeting is allowed.

**Offer copy must carry its conditions in the ad (LFPC Art. 48).** State the eligibility, what the offer applies to (inscripción or colegiatura), the deadline, whether it combines with other offers, and the total cost (Art. 7 Bis). Example:
> "Beca institucional de hasta 40% en colegiatura con promedio mínimo de 8.5; nuevo ingreso; inscripciones hasta el 30/11/2026; no acumulable."

This is required, and it also protects the client now that PROFECO's jurisdiction is SCJN-confirmed. Distinguish these:
- institutional "becas", which are commercial discounts;
- the legal 5% scholarships (Art. 149);
- state scholarships for private basic schools.

Do not call a discount a "beca SEP".

**Claims to avoid or substantiate:**
- **"Título en 6 meses" / "titúlate rápido":** defensible only for Acuerdo 286 candidates or people who already completed their credits. Disclose the requirements (for example, at least 50% of credits for regulated degrees, CENEVAL fees). Otherwise it fits PROFECO's "inexact, exaggerated, partial" test for misleading advertising (see the sibling notes).
- **"Certifica tu prepa en un examen":**
  - This is a real route: CENEVAL's Acredita-Bach under Acuerdo 286, with the certificate issued digitally by the SEP's DGB.
  - A private advertiser that sells preparation should say "Te preparamos para el examen CENEVAL (Acuerdo 286)".
  - It must not imply that it issues the certificate or that passing is guaranteed.
  - Minors need parental-consent paperwork.
- **"Titulación automática":** acceptable when the RVOE plan includes the thesis substitute. Better wording: "titulación sin tesis incluida en el plan de estudios", next to the RVOE legend.
- **Employability:** never "empleo garantizado"; see the Meta "unrealistic outcomes" policy in section 2. Use only figures with a source and date (cohort, survey size).

**Minors and data (precautionary, since the 2025 minors text is unconfirmed).**
- Treat any lead under 18 as needing parental or guardian consent.
- K-12 forms: make the parent the respondent ("Soy madre / padre / tutor").
- Prepa and university forms: add "¿Eres mayor de edad?" and route minors to a parent-consent step, or run lead ads to 18+ only.
- Collect the child's grade or level of interest rather than detailed child data.
- Any student photo or testimonial of a minor in a creative needs written parental consent and the child's opinion (the LGDNNA Art. 78 logic). Adult models or real parents are lower risk.

**K-12 and bachillerato ads (1992 agreement and PROFECO).**
- The ad can promise "costos claros por escrito"; the follow-up must deliver a written cost sheet before enrollment.
- Claims such as "sin cuotas extra" or "uniformes en el proveedor que elijas" are trust signals, but only if true.

## 2. Meta targeting and policy constraints relevant in Mexico

### Takeaway
Education is not a Meta special ad category, so normal targeting applies. Four constraints still matter:
- **Under-18 targeting:** since February 2023, Meta reaches under-18s only by age and location (no gender, no interests or activity).
- **Personal attributes:** copy must not assert or imply them, and the list explicitly includes **age** and **vulnerable financial status**.
- **Unrealistic outcomes:** Meta's policy bars guaranteed employment or income without specifics.
- **Lead-form sensitive data:** for example government IDs such as CURP, financial or health data. The only source found is a third-party tracker that also logs a July 2026 change, so check it in Ads Manager.

- **Teens (since February 2023):**
  - Meta removed gender as a targeting option for users under 18 on Facebook and Instagram.
  - Advertisers can no longer target teens based on in-app activity (Pages liked, accounts followed).
  - Age and location are the only information used to show ads to teens.
  - Teens got "Ad Topic Controls".

### Inferences
**K-12 and prepa.**
- Make parents the primary lead-form audience, typically adults about 25-55, with Advantage+ or broad targeting and parent-oriented copy.
- Run lead-form ad sets at **18+**. Meta limits teen targeting to age and location, and teen leads raise the parental-consent question in section 1.
- Do not rely on retargeting or lookalikes to reach under-18s; Meta says it uses only age and location for teens.

**17-19-year-old prospects.**
- Under 18: age and location only; prefer awareness video and content.
- 18-19: normal targeting.
- Time campaigns to the public-admission result dates in section 3 rather than to interests.

**Copy rules for maquetas (personal attributes).**
- Avoid "¿Tienes más de 30 y no terminaste tu carrera?" (asserts age). Prefer "Termina tu licenciatura en línea mientras trabajas."
- Avoid "¿No te alcanza para la universidad?" or "¿Tienes deudas?" (vulnerable financial status). Prefer "Becas de hasta X% según tu promedio. Consulta condiciones."
- Avoid "¿Tienes TDAH? Estudia a tu ritmo" (health). Prefer "Prepa en línea a tu ritmo."
- **Borderline:** "¿No te quedaste en la UNAM?". Education status is not on Meta's list, but second-person framing can trigger review. Test it against a neutral version: "Inscripciones abiertas para septiembre: licenciaturas con RVOE."

**Outcome claims.** Avoid "empleo garantizado", "gana $X al egresar" and "100% de colocación". Use dated, sourced statistics with a footnote ("X% de egresados 2024 laborando a 6 meses; encuesta interna n=___"), or describe the services instead ("bolsa de trabajo", "convenios con empresas").

**Lead-form fields.**
- Do not ask for CURP (a government ID), income or "¿cuánto puedes pagar?" (financial), or disability or health questions in Meta forms.
- Collect those later in admissions, under the full privacy notice, if a means-tested scholarship needs them.

**Optimization.** Use conversion-leads optimization with CRM-stage feedback (Conversions API) when the client has a CRM. Meta's own 2023 Latin America data shows the cost per quality lead improving.

## 4. Typical offers and trust signals in Mexican education ads

### Takeaway
The strongest trust signal is now a verifiable RVOE per program and per address. In 2026 the SEP, PROFECO and state authorities shut or flagged unrecognized "universidades", and the legend is mandatory anyway (LGE Art. 148).

Next come transparent costs and scholarship conditions, now that the SCJN has confirmed PROFECO's power to resolve complaints.

Offers that are sourced, though weakly:
- merit-based scholarships of 10-45% (UNITEC, undated third-party document);
- a free first month of tuition (UNITEC, same document);
- year-round admissions and tetramestral starts (Tecmilenio);
- executive degrees in 8 cuatrimestres, semi-in-person or with one class a week (UTEL, UNITEC);
- titles without a thesis ("titulación automática").

Not sourced: no 2026 examples of "inscripción $0", "colegiatura congelada" (frozen tuition) or MSI (*meses sin intereses*, interest-free instalments) turned up.

Free public options (UnADM, Prepa en Línea-SEP) are the benchmark that paid online programs are compared with.

### Inferences
**Trust-signal stack for maquetas (education), in order of priority:**
1. The full RVOE or incorporation legend (LGE Art. 148), plus "Verifica nuestro RVOE en SIRVOES (SEP)", or the state registry for state RVOE.
2. The program-specific RVOE agreement number on the card that advertises that program.
3. A real campus address or map pin, which matters because the legend must name the authorized address.
4. A clear modality statement: "clases en vivo" only if they are really synchronous; "100% en línea", "mixta" or "sabatina" as applicable.
5. Costs: "colegiatura desde $X; inscripción $Y; costo total del programa $Z (IVA incluido si aplica)", with scholarship and promotion conditions and a deadline.
6. Duration and starts: "8 cuatrimestres", "inicio 18 de enero de 2027", "inicios cada [cuatrimestre/mes]" (only if true).
7. Credentials of the faculty and the institution. Accreditations such as FIMPES are a plausible signal but were not researched.
8. Real adult testimonials: alumni, working-adult students, or parents for K-12.
9. Payment flexibility: MSI and card or OXXO payments (see the sibling notes).
10. Outcomes: only dated, sourced employability statistics.

**Offer layer by vertical** (to be tested; based on the weakly sourced market examples above):
- **Universities and online degrees:** merit scholarship by grade average ("hasta X%"); a first month or enrollment at reduced or zero cost with a deadline; a clear start date; RVOE.
- **Licenciatura ejecutiva:** duration in cuatrimestres; one class a week or 100% online; credit transfer (*revalidación de materias*), not researched; thesis-free title included in the plan.
- **Prepa and bachillerato:**
  - Private prepa: compete on support and modality against free Prepa en Línea-SEP ("asesor personal", "clases en vivo").
  - Acuerdo 286 prep providers: "prepárate para el examen CENEVAL", never "te damos el certificado".
- **K-12:** "costos claros por escrito", no compulsory extra fees, open house or visit booking, becas.
- **Language schools, diplomados and bootcamps:**
  - Placement test, start cohorts and schedule (not sourced).
  - If the course has no RVOE, include "Estudios sin reconocimiento de validez oficial" as a precaution (scope unclear; see section 1 Gaps).
  - Bootcamps must avoid guaranteed-job claims (Meta and LFPC).

**Competitive positioning.** Free public options exist (UnADM, Prepa en Línea-SEP), so paid online-program ads should state what the fee buys: flexible starts, no admission exam or waiting list, advising, speed, live classes. They should not imply the public options lack validity, because they have it.

- No 2026 sourced examples were found of "inscripción $0", "colegiatura congelada", MSI on tuition, "clases en vivo" claims or employability claims by Mexican universities.
- FIMPES, ANUIES and other accreditation or ranking trust signals were not researched.
- UVM's 2026 offers were not found.

## 5. Lead-funnel practices for Mexican education

### Takeaway
Mexico-specific evidence is thin and mostly vendor-sourced:
- A Mexico-based online university, Universidad UK, uses click-to-WhatsApp ads that open a conversation with an advisor. It added WhatsApp voice calling through Twilio to raise response rates and shorten time to enrollment. Meta's published story gives no enrollment figures.
- Meta reports 16% lower cost per quality lead from conversion-leads optimization with CRM feedback (Latin America, 2023).
- US higher-education data suggests replying within 5 minutes lifts conversion; the vendor figure is 28%.

Education CPL benchmarks are US or global (about US$19-28) or come from Mexican agency blogs (MXN 100-400 per action, in the sibling notes). All are low-reliability. Plan on each client's own cost per qualified lead and cost per enrolled student.

### Cited Findings
- **Universidad UK (a Mexico-based online university):**
  - It usually advertises its programs with click-to-WhatsApp ads that open a conversation with a representative.
  - It partnered with Twilio to use the WhatsApp Business API voice-call feature, which made it easier for prospects to call from the chat and raised response rates.
  - The goal was to shorten the time to convert prospects from digital campaigns.

### Inferences
**Funnel choice by vertical:**
- **Universities, online degrees and ejecutivas:**
  - Run a "higher intent" instant form with three to five qualifying questions.
  - Then send an automatic WhatsApp template within a minute, and have an advisor call or chat within 5 minutes in business hours (the AI agent covers off-hours; see the sibling notes).
  - Run click-to-WhatsApp in parallel for prospects ready to talk now. A WhatsApp Business API voice-call button follows the Universidad UK pattern.
  - Send the RVOE agreement and a written cost sheet in the first conversation. This builds trust and satisfies the cost-disclosure logic of the 1992 agreement for prepa and K-12.
- **K-12:** the goal is a campus visit or open house. Use an instant form or click-to-WhatsApp, with the parent as respondent, and send costs in writing before enrollment.
- **Prepa (adult or online) and Acuerdo 286 prep:** click-to-WhatsApp works well for answering questions about eligibility (finished secundaria, age, CENEVAL dates).

**Qualifying questions** (no sourced education-specific guidance; drafts, avoiding sensitive fields):
- *Universities and ejecutivas:*
  - program of interest;
  - modality (en línea / mixta / presencial);
  - desired start (enero / mayo / septiembre);
  - "¿Cuentas con certificado de bachillerato?" (sí / en trámite / no);
  - "¿Estudias o trabajas actualmente?";
  - preferred schedule;
  - state or campus;
  - best time and channel for contact.
- *Prepa:*
  - "¿Quién llena este formulario?" (aspirante mayor de edad / madre-padre-tutor);
  - "¿Concluiste la secundaria?";
  - modality (en línea / sabatina / examen CENEVAL).
- *K-12:*
  - respondent role;
  - grade the child will enter in 2027-28;
  - campus;
  - preferred visit date.
- *Language schools:* language; current level; goal (trabajo / certificación / viaje); schedule; modality.
- *Bootcamps and diplomados:* area; background; schedule; interest in financing. Never ask for income.
- **Do not ask in Meta forms:** CURP, income or ability to pay, health or disability details.

**Speed-to-lead SLA for clients:**
- First touch within 5 minutes during staffed hours, and an instant automated WhatsApp reply 24/7.
- Track median time to first contact per advisor.
- The speed data is US vendor data, so treat it as directional, but this is still the cheapest lever available.

**Metrics:** CPL alone misleads in education; Meta's cheaper on-platform leads are colder. Report:
- cost per qualified lead;
- cost per interview or visit;
