## 1. Alex Hormozi: ad structure, lead magnets, value equation / Grand Slam Offer, ad volume and creative testing, published templates

### Takeaway
Hormozi's paid-ad anatomy in *$100M Leads* (2023) has three parts: **Callout -> Value -> Call to Action**. The ad's offer is usually a lead magnet of one of three types: reveal their problem, sample/trial, or one step of a multi-step process. The value is built with the **Value Equation** from *$100M Offers*. In 2025 he added two things: an aggressive creative-volume system (hook multiplication, a 70/20/10 "more / adjacent / new" split, organic pre-testing of hooks) and the "attraction offers" from *$100M Money Models* (2025). "Hook-Retain-Reward" is his framework for **free content**, not his ad formula. Practitioners re-map it onto video ads as hook / body ("meat") / CTA.

### Cited Findings

#### Ad structure ("what to say in an ad")
- In the paid-ads chapter of *$100M Leads* (released as audiobook episodes on Hormozi's podcast, "Part 6 - Run Paid Ads, Ep 591"), an ad has three parts: call-outs, value elements and calls to action. Call-outs are labels that sort people by traits, titles or places. The CTA tells the reader exactly what to do next, e.g., click a button, call a number or scan a QR code. Source: [Deciphr summary of The Game Ep 591](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-6--run-paid-ads-ep-591). This is a third-party summary of Hormozi's own audio, so the wording may differ from the book.
- Reading notes on the book list these callout tactics:
  - labels for groups of people
  - "local area + type of person" callouts
  - yes/no questions that make the right people self-identify
  - "If... then..." statements

  The notes also recommend producing many callout variations and using a simple landing page that matches the ad. Source: [Max Mednik, Notes on $100M Leads](https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-leads-by-alex-hormozi) (secondary).
- Summaries describe the value element in two ways: as a "What-Who-When" framing, and as an application of the value equation (raise the dream outcome and the belief it will work; lower time delay and effort). Sobrief says the value equation is used throughout the book for offers, ads and lead magnets. Sources: [Summarypedia](https://summarypedia.org/100m-leads/); [Sobrief](https://sobrief.com/books/100m-leads). I could not confirm from the extracts which summary is the origin of "What-Who-When."
- Paid ads are one of the "Core Four" ways to get leads in *$100M Leads*: warm outreach, free content, cold outreach and paid ads. Source: [Business BookClub summary](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/free-book-summary-100m-leads-by-alex).

#### Hook-Retain-Reward (a content framework, often borrowed for video ads)
- The book's back-cover blurb lists a "hook-retain-reward system" for turning content into leads. Per reading notes:
  - **Hook** = topics plus headlines. A headline's job is to give a reason to keep going.
  - **Retain** = lists, steps and stories.
  - **Reward** = the payoff that satisfies the reason they were hooked.

  Sources: [Max Mednik notes](https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-leads-by-alex-hormozi); [personal Obsidian notes, "Hook, Retain, Reward"](https://publish.obsidian.md/charllaubscher/05+Love+%2B+Money/03+Process/Processes+for+Partnerships/Sales/Hook%2C+Retain%2C+Reward).
- A creative-framework library applies hook/retain/reward to short-form video ads without crediting Hormozi. It says most ads die because they nail the hook and then sag. Source: [Selfstorming](https://www.selfstorming.com/tools/libraries/frameworks/hook-retain-reward).
- A third-party "Hook-Meat-CTA" framing attributed to Hormozi: keep the meat (body) and CTA roughly constant, rotate hooks, and treat the hook as the experiment. Source: [Gavel, "Alex Hormozi's Hook-Meat-CTA Ad Framework"](https://usegavel.com/alex-hormozi/hook-meat-cta). This is secondary, not a Hormozi publication.

#### Lead magnets (the usual ad offer)
- *$100M Leads* gives three types of lead magnet:
  1. **Reveal their problem.** Diagnose a problem they don't know they have, which creates urgency.
  2. **Samples and trials.** A taste of the core offer.
  3. **One step of a multi-step process.** One piece of the solution, so they want the rest.

  Sources: [Glasp book highlights](https://glasp.co/discover/book/B0CFDR3TYV); [Max Mednik notes](https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-leads-by-alex-hormozi).
- Summaries paraphrase his definition of a lead magnet as a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next problem, which the paid offer solves. The same search results included a summary of his 2025-11-18 episode, which I could not open to see what it adds. Source: [Shortform summary of The Game Ep 969, "Generate 1000s of Leads (In Any Niche)"](https://shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-11-18-episode-summary-generate-1000s-of-leads-in-any-niche-ep-969).

#### Value Equation and offer framing
- Formula: **Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)**, from *$100M Offers*.
  - Raise the numerator with a vivid outcome, social proof and guarantees/risk reversal.
  - Push the denominator toward zero with fast deliverables, quick wins and done-for-you parts.

  Sources: [Hormozi Wiki](https://alexhormozi.wiki/frameworks/value-equation-explained); [Medium explainer](https://medium.com/@loreviglietti/value-equation-formulated-by-alex-hormozi-5dea4915897a).
- Hormozi's stated goal for an offer is the *$100M Offers* subtitle, used as the title of his interview: "How To Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No." Source: [Young and Profiting, E199](https://youngandprofiting.com/alex-hormozi-the-value-equation-how-to-make-offers-so-good-people-feel-stupid-saying-no-e199/).

#### *$100M Money Models* (2025): attraction offers that can serve as lead-gen ad offers
- The book has five "attraction offers":
  - **Win Your Money Back.** The customer hits a set goal and gets a refund or credit. A summary traces the idea to a gym.
  - **Giveaways.** A grand prize in exchange for contact info. One winner is drawn, and everyone else is offered the product at a discount.
  - **Decoy Offer.** A stripped-down free or cheap option next to a premium one. One summary reports about an 80% take rate when a free basic gym membership sat beside a $399 premium package. I could not confirm that figure against the book.
  - **Buy X Get Y Free.** Example: "Buy 1 Pair Get Two Pair Free" at $600.
  - **Pay Less Now or Pay More Later.**

  Sources: [SuperSummary](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/); [Business BookClub](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/100m-money-models-by-alex-hormozi); [Howtoes summary, 2026-07-24](https://howtoes.blog/2026/07/24/100m-money-models-alex-hormozi-complete-book-summary-all-key-ideas/); book listing: [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/240367486-100m-money-models).

#### Creative volume and testing (2025-2026)
- In a 2025-05-03 episode, Hormozi argues for very high creative volume: about 50 pieces a day, made by multiplying one message across different hooks. In one example a single recording session produced about 150 ads by testing angles. He describes a 70/20/10 split:
  - 70% of future creative built on the historically best hooks
  - 20% on adjacent ideas
  - 10% on wild ideas

  Source: [Shortform summary, The Game Ep 878 "Brutally Honest Business Advice"](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-05-03-episode-summary-brutally-honest-business-advice-ep-878). This is a third-party summary; I did not check the 50/day and 150-ad figures against the audio.
- A review of an Acquisition.com workshop describes the same 70/20/10 split as three buckets:
  - "more": variations of winners
  - "adjacent": a relaunch with a new hook, a new CTA or new social proof
  - "new": experiments

  The variables that matter are a "new hook" (first 3-5 seconds), a "new CTA" (last 5-7 seconds) and new or changed social proof. Source: [Art & Biz Substack, VAM workshop review](https://artandbiz.substack.com/p/alex-hormozi-vam-workshop-review) (undated in the extract).
- Hormozi advises testing hooks organically first (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), so paid budget goes only to creative that has already proven itself. Source: [Forbes (Jodie Cook), URL-dated 2026-09-18](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jodiecook/2026/09/18/alex-hormozis-8-rules-for-creating-more-demand-than-you-can-handle/). The search extract called it a "June 2026" piece, but the URL date is 2026-09-18.
- A Shortform summary attributes to Hormozi the view that the hook can be about 80% of an ad's effectiveness. Source: [Shortform Ep 878 summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-05-03-episode-summary-brutally-honest-business-advice-ep-878). I'm not sure the claim comes from this specific episode.
- Hormozi released "The Funnel We Use to Make $250M+" (Ep 898) on 2025-06-02. I could not retrieve its contents. Source: [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-06-02-episode-summary-the-funnel-we-use-to-make-250m-ep-898).
- Hormozi launched *$100M Leads* with a webinar. A third-party script breakdown reports "$4.4M in 90 minutes" (self-reported revenue). Source: [AmpiFire](https://ampifire.com/blog/alex-hormozi-persuasion-techniques-100m-leads-launch/).

Examples: [LobeHub hormozi-goated-ads](https://lobehub.com/skills/itsar-vr-goatedskills-hormozi-goated-ads), [MCP Market Hormozi Ad Factory](https://mcpmarket.com/tools/skills/hormozi-ad-factory-2).

### Inferences
**Recipe H1: Hormozi "Callout -> Value -> CTA" ad with a lead-magnet offer.** Best for local services, professional/B2B services and education.
- **Ad skeleton** (built from the cited parts; not a verbatim Hormozi template):
  - **Callout:** "[Local area] + [type of person]" / a yes-no question that makes the right buyer self-identify / "If you [situation], then..."
  - **Value:** the dream outcome, plus proof (likelihood), plus speed (time), plus low effort. Say what they get, who it's for and when they get it.
  - **CTA:** one explicit action ("Tap the button and fill out the form", "Call now", "Scan the QR code").
- **Offer:**
  - Type 1, reveal the problem: free inspection, audit, diagnosis or assessment.
  - Type 2, sample/trial: trial class, first session, sample deliverable.
  - Type 3, one step: a plan, a calculator, a template.
- **Destination:** an instant form, or a simple landing page that matches the ad (section 4 explains how to choose).
- **After Andromeda:** still valid, and arguably more important. If creative now does much of the audience matching (see section 6), each distinct callout (persona, place, situation) works like a separate audience. Write callouts as conceptually different ads, not tiny copy tweaks.
- **MX template (my adaptation), B2B:** "Despachos contables en Monterrey con 3 a 15 empleados: en una auditoría de 30 minutos sin costo te mostramos dónde se están perdiendo prospectos por WhatsApp y qué cambiar para [resultado medible y comprobable]. Toca 'Más información' y llena el formulario; te contactamos hoy mismo."
- **MX template (my adaptation), local services:** "Vecinos de Zapopan y Tlajomulco: revisión de azotea GRATIS antes de lluvias (sin compromiso). En 20 minutos sabrás si hay filtraciones y cuánto costaría repararlas. Agenda en el formulario."

**Recipe H2: the Hormozi creative engine, adapted to Andromeda.**
- Record one strong body (meat) and one CTA.
- Shoot many hooks (callouts, questions, "If... then") and pre-test them organically.
- Keep the 70/20/10 "more / adjacent / new" allocation.
- In the "new" bucket, change persona, angle and format, not just the first line. The vendor guidance in section 6 says Andromeda rewards conceptual diversity.

**Recipe H3: Money Models attraction offers mapped to Superleads verticals.** These are inferences; check compliance before use.
- **Fitness/gyms:** "Win your money back" challenge, e.g., a refund or credit if attendance and goal criteria are met.
- **Clinics, aesthetics, education:** "Decoy" pairing, e.g., a free basic assessment next to a premium package.
- **Education:** "Buy X get Y" (course plus a free module or mentoring).
- **Giveaways:** use with caution in Mexico. Sweepstakes ("sorteos") may need a government permit (see Gaps), and Meta's promotion rules apply.

## 2. What the other gurus actually teach about Meta ads and lead gen (2024-2026): Brunson, Haynes, Suby, Piliero, Dan Henry, Cole Gordon, Deiss/DigitalMarketer, Pittman/Firestone, others

### Takeaway
The most concrete and best-dated public Meta lead-gen system for high-ticket offers in 2025-2026 comes from **Jeremy Haynes (Megalodon)**:
- no-CTA "content ads" that warm up cold traffic
- direct-response ads that feed a **VSL call funnel**: headline -> VSL or mini-webinar -> application with conditional logic -> calendar embedded on the same page
- a cheaper instant-form "application" called **Hydra** for smaller budgets
- **"Hammer Them"**: content injected between every funnel step

Brunson's Hook-Story-Offer and Suby's HVCO -> Godfather Offer -> Magic Lantern sequence remain the copy and offer backbones. Both come from older books but are still widely used. Piliero's material is DTC-focused, Dan Henry's webinar and value-video funnels are older, and Cole Gordon works on the sales/setting side; none of them adds much lead-gen-specific Meta ad content. I could not research Deiss/DigitalMarketer or Pittman/Firestone in this session.

### Cited Findings

- **Hydra** is a lead-form (instant form) strategy that uses conditional logic the way an application would, and hands off qualified applicants. Haynes says it suits smaller budgets because cost per lead is usually cheaper than a call funnel. Results are mixed: it worked for some capital-raising clients and failed for others. Sources: Haynes posts that came up with the tier list ([X tier-list post](https://x.com/TheJeremyHaynes/status/1913238232818479516); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeremy-haynes-6b5476a3/)).   Source: [X](https://x.com/TheJeremyHaynes/status/1919398782455677109) (self-reported).

He also says "hiding the price actually kills your conversions with wealthy prospects." Source: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeremy-haynes-6b5476a3/).

#### Russell Brunson (ClickFunnels)
- **Hook-Story-Offer** (from *DotCom Secrets*): a hook pulls attention, a story shows a relatable change, and an offer invites action. Source: [Write With AI Substack](https://writewithai.substack.com/p/hook-story-offer-machine-the-easiest).

## 3. Local-service and vertical templates (home services, dental, med-spa/aesthetics, real estate, law, fitness, education, B2B) and cautions for regulated industries

### Takeaway
I could not verify who originated the widely copied "We're looking for [X] homeowners in [City] who want [result]" ad, and the phrase did not appear in any 2025-2026 guide I found. Current contractor guides converge on a different pattern:
- free inspection or estimate offers in "no pressure" language
- instant forms for urgent or storm-driven demand
- before/after carousels for retail jobs

#### Cautions for regulated industries, relevant to these templates
- **Personal attributes.** Ads cannot assert or imply that the advertiser knows a viewer's personal attributes (health, body, finances, etc.), and this includes indirect implications. Meta examples as quoted by vendors:
  - allowed: "Depression counseling" and "New diabetes treatment available"
  - rejected: "Do you have diabetes?"
  - fine, because "you" is not tied to an attribute: "Find a therapist near you", "Book your appointment today", "Get your free guide"
  - flagged, because it implies knowledge of a family member's health: "Get your spouse treated for cancer today"

  Sources: [Zappush](https://www.zappush.com/blog/meta-personal-attributes-policy-health-wellness-ads); [Curve Compliance](https://www.curvecompliance.com/ad-account-rescue/meta-ad-rejected-personal-attributes); [WeTracked](https://www.wetracked.io/post/meta-ads-new-sensitive-categories-restrictions). These are vendor blogs quoting Meta; I did not open Meta's own policy page.
- **Rewrite pattern: describe the service, not the reader.** Examples: "Diabetes treatment and monitoring services are available"; for dental, "Learn about cosmetic and restorative dental options for a more confident smile." Sources: [Zappush](https://www.zappush.com/blog/meta-personal-attributes-policy-health-wellness-ads); [Primores](https://primores.org/wiki/marketing/meta-ad-policy/).
- **Instant-form questions** must also avoid "you/your" plus a personal attribute. Ask about the product, timeline or budget instead. Source: instant-form guides, e.g., [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-instant-form).
- **Before/after.** Vendor policy tables flag side-by-side transformation comparisons that show idealized results (a slimmer "after" next to a heavier "before"), and close-ups of body parts that reinforce insecurity. Sources: [AuditSocials, 2026](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ad-misleading-claims-personal-attributes-prohibited-content-policy-2026); [Primores](https://primores.org/wiki/marketing/meta-ad-policy/).
- A vendor claims that enforcement in March 2026 extended to indirect second-person framing, such as "For people managing diabetes." This is unverified. Source: [AuditSocials, 2026 policy updates](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ad-policy-updates-2026-guide).
- **Health & wellness data restrictions (from January 2025).** Health and wellness advertisers can no longer optimize for lower-funnel events such as Purchase and Add to Cart; some sources add Booked Appointment. They are steered toward landing page views, lead forms, engagement and video views.
  - Matchnode describes three tiers: Core Setup, Mid-Restricted and Fully Restricted.
  - Pixel and Conversions API data are also restricted.
  - The rollout continues through 2026.

  Sources: [Matchnode](https://matchnode.com/metas-new-data-restrictions/); [Polar Analytics](https://www.polaranalytics.com/post/2025-metas-tracking-restrictions-for-health-wellness-are-here----heres-how-to-fix-it); [Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/metas-health-ad-policy-updates-what-changed); [Faebl Studios](https://faeblstudios.com/resources/meta-ad-2025-2026-restrictions-what-behavioral-health-providers-need-to-know/).
- One source claims fitness services and health clubs are exempt from the age-targeting restrictions (unverified). Source: [Elmt](https://www.elmtmarketing.com/blog/meta-ads).

## 4. Funnel destinations and qualification; how top practitioners fix low lead quality (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Instant forms remain the volume engine in 2025-2026, but lead quality now comes from layers:
1. The **Higher Intent** form type plus a few qualifying questions with conditional logic, and a separate "non-lead" ending.
2. Meta's late-2025 **verification tools**: SMS phone verification, a required work email, and address validation (still a test).
3. Most important: sending CRM stages back through **Conversions API for CRM** and optimizing for **conversion leads**. Requirements include at least 200 leads a month and a target stage reached within 28 days.

For high ticket, Haynes prefers VSL -> application with conditional logic -> embedded calendar, and uses instant-form applications (Hydra) when budgets are small. Messaging ads use short Q&A flows that ask easy questions first and hand off to a human.

### Cited Findings

#### Instant form types and anatomy
- There are three form types:
  - **More Volume:** the default; the fastest to complete on mobile.
  - **Higher Intent:** adds a step where people review and confirm their information before submitting, which means fewer accidental submissions and more interested leads.
  - **Rich Creative:** allows more messaging on the form itself.

  Sources: [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/instant-forms/); [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-capture-more-qualified-leads-with-facebook-ads/); [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-instant-form).
- Claimed trade-offs (not verified against Meta data): Higher Intent cuts volume by roughly 20-40%. Contact rates are 40-65% for More Volume versus 70-85% for Higher Intent. Sources: [GrowWithSakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-lead-ads/); [Upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/facebook-lead-ads-fake-leads-never-filled-form/). The extract did not say which of the two gives which number.
- Guidance on which type to use:
  - Higher Intent for service inquiries where sales follow-up is costly.
  - More Volume for content downloads, webinar registrations and newsletter signups.
  - Consider Higher Intent unless you have a proven way to qualify leads efficiently.

  Sources: same search results ([GrowWithSakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-lead-ads/); [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-instant-form)).
- Form anatomy:
  - an optional intro, the questions, a required privacy-policy link, and an ending (headline, message, CTA button)
  - question types: multiple choice, short answer, appointment request, store locator
  - up to 15 custom questions

  Sources: [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-instant-form); [LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/facebook-instant-forms-vs-website-forms/); [PPC Hero](https://ppchero.com/a-walkthrough-guide-to-meta-instant-form-ads/).
- **Conditional logic:**
  - It is set per custom question and is available only in Ads Manager.
  - An answer can jump to another question, submit the form or close it.
  - Optional questions cannot be used when conditional logic is on.
  - With logic there are two endings, one for leads and one for non-leads.
  - Guides suggest that disqualifying answers end the form or show a "not a fit" message (e.g., homeowners only).

  Sources: [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-capture-more-qualified-leads-with-facebook-ads/); [Medium (Aquib)](https://medium.com/@aquib.9011/how-to-build-a-facebook-instant-form-that-filters-out-everyone-who-isnt-serious-d1270f6ae4fe); [LeadsBridge](https://leadsbridge.com/blog/instant-forms-example/).

### Inferences
**Recipe Q1: quality stack for Meta lead gen in 2026.** Apply in this order.
1. **Form:** Higher Intent plus 2-4 qualifying questions with conditional logic, and a separate non-lead ending. Questions ask about the product, timeline and budget, never personal attributes.
2. **Verification:** SMS phone verification for consumer verticals. Require a work email for B2B where available (Instagram or API).
3. **Speed:** a setter or WhatsApp follow-up within minutes. The ending screen says who will call, from which number and when.
4. **Signal:** CRM -> Conversions API for CRM, optimizing for a stage reached within 28 days, e.g., "contacted and qualified" or "appointment attended." This needs at least about 200 leads a month per account. Smaller clients should pool volume or stay on standard leads optimization plus manual scoring.
5. **Campaign:** test Advantage+ leads with website and instant forms in the same campaign; Meta claims 14-24% lower CPL versus website forms alone.
6. **Lead scoring:** use the form answers (role, budget band, urgency) to route hot leads to a closer and the rest to nurture. Haynes's "Hammer Them" content between steps suits the nurture leg.

**Instant-form copy templates (my adaptations, for Mexico):**
- **Intro:** headline "Cotización sin costo en 2 minutos — [servicio] en [ciudad]". Three bullets: what they get / who it is for (service area, minimum job size) / what happens next ("te llamamos hoy desde el 33-XXXX-XXXX").
- **Q1** "¿Qué servicio necesitas?" (multiple choice).
- **Q2** "¿En qué municipio/colonia es el trabajo?" Out-of-area answers go to the non-lead ending.
- **Q3** "¿Para cuándo lo necesitas?" (Esta semana / Este mes / 1–3 meses / Solo estoy comparando).
- **Q4** "Presupuesto aproximado" (ranges; the lowest goes to nurture).
- **Q5 (B2B only)** "¿Cuál es tu rol?" (Dueño/Director / Gerente / Otro).
- **Lead ending:** "¡Listo! Un asesor te llamará en los próximos 15 minutos desde el [número]. Si prefieres, escríbenos por WhatsApp aquí." CTA: WhatsApp or the website.
- **Non-lead ending:** "Gracias. Por ahora atendemos solo [zona/criterio]. Te dejamos nuestra guía gratuita de [tema] por si te sirve." This keeps goodwill and still offers a type-1 or type-3 lead magnet.

**Recipe Q2: click-to-WhatsApp qualification flow,** following Meta's advice to ask easy questions first.
- Ask, in order: service (quick replies) -> zone -> timing -> budget (sensitive, so last) -> offer two appointment slots or hand off to a human.
- Above about 50 conversations a week, automate through the WhatsApp Business API and push stages to the CRM. Mark the conversion stage for CAPI where the setup supports it.

**How to choose the destination (synthesis):**

| Situation | Destination |
|---|---|
| Low-ticket local service or urgent need | Instant form (Higher Intent) or click-to-WhatsApp |
| Considered purchase (remodeling, education, aesthetics) | Lead magnet (HVCO) -> nurture -> booking |
| High-ticket B2B, consulting, coaching | VSL -> application -> calendar (JH1); Hydra if the budget is under about [X] |

- I did not research **quiz funnels**, **click-to-call** ("Calls" ads), or the 2025-2026 specifics of **webinar and challenge** funnels (beyond Haynes's comment on show rates and Dan Henry's older material).
- **Speed-to-lead studies** were not retrieved. Unverified background, to verify before citing: a 2011 HBR article, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads," reported that firms contacting leads within an hour were about 7x more likely to qualify them.

## 5. Offer frameworks used in lead-gen ads (free audit/consultation, lead magnets, trials, discounts, guarantees, scarcity/urgency, bonuses, price anchoring), with copy templates

### Takeaway
The offers that recur across these gurus fall into a few families:
- diagnostic lead magnets: Hormozi's "reveal their problem," Suby's HVCO
- free samples, trials and consultations: Hormozi's type 2, the "no pressure" free inspection in contractor guides
- one-step tools: Hormozi's type 3
- front-end "attraction offers" from *Money Models* (2025): win-your-money-back, decoy, giveaway, buy-X-get-Y

All of them are engineered with the Value Equation: raise proof and guarantees, lower time and effort. High-ticket players add price transparency (Haynes), "new opportunity" framing and value stacking (Brunson), and "bold promise" Godfather offers (Suby). Few verbatim, published ad-copy templates were accessible. Most "templates" in circulation are reconstructions by third parties.

### Cited Findings
- **Lead-magnet families** (Hormozi): reveal their problem / samples and trials / one step of a multi-step process. Sources: [Glasp](https://glasp.co/discover/book/B0CFDR3TYV); [Max Mednik](https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-leads-by-alex-hormozi).
- **Value Equation levers:** guarantees and social proof raise perceived likelihood; quick wins and done-for-you parts lower time and effort. Sources: [Hormozi Wiki](https://alexhormozi.wiki/frameworks/value-equation-explained); [Medium](https://medium.com/@loreviglietti/value-equation-formulated-by-alex-hormozi-5dea4915897a).
- **Attraction offers** (*$100M Money Models*, 2025): Win Your Money Back (a conditional guarantee); Giveaway (grand prize plus a discount for non-winners); Decoy (free or basic option next to premium, i.e., price anchoring); Buy X Get Y Free; Pay Less Now or Pay More Later. Sources: [SuperSummary](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/); [Business BookClub](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/100m-money-models-by-alex-hormozi).
- **Godfather Offer** (Suby): an irresistible offer built on big claims and bold promises. **HVCO**: a free report, video or cheat sheet as the lead magnet for paid traffic. Sources: [Goodreads notes](https://www.goodreads.com/notes/43787527-sell-like-crazy/77040939-benjamin-arya/e4c9bb01-c194-4fdc-b460-b909242bd2c1); [HumanisedTech](https://www.humanisedtech.com/sell-like-crazy-desconstructed/sabri-subys-magic-lantern-technique/).
- **The book as lead magnet:** Suby's April 2025 Meta ad offers a free copy of *Sell Like Crazy*. Source: [AdScan](https://adscan.ai/ad/1020786462720198).
- **"New opportunity" over "improvement"** offers, and the **stack slide** for value and bonuses (Brunson). Source: [Shortform blog](https://shortform.com/blog/?p=129769).
- **Offer quality drives funnel economics** (Haynes, 2025-12-29): "Bad offer = high cost per call, long sales cycles, low show rates, poor close rates • Good offer = low cost per call, high show rates, fast closes, max profitability". Source: [X](https://x.com/TheJeremyHaynes/status/2005662148127502601).
- **Price transparency:** "hiding the price actually kills your conversions with wealthy prospects" (Haynes). Source: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeremy-haynes-6b5476a3/).
- **Free inspection with "no pressure" risk reversal** (roofing storm campaigns, 2026). Source: [BuiltRight Digital](https://builtrightdigital.com/roofing-meta-ads-guide/).
- **Cheat-sheet, discount and value-video funnels** (Dan Henry; older material). Source: [course curriculum listing](https://howcourses.it.com/elearning/dan-henry-facebook-ads-for-entrepreneurs/).
- **An explicit, single CTA** ("click a button, call a number, or scan a QR code"; "Fill out the form below"). Sources: [Deciphr Ep 591](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-6--run-paid-ads-ep-591); [Summarypedia](https://summarypedia.org/100m-leads/).

### Inferences
**How offers map to business types (synthesis):**

| Offer | Where it fits |
|---|---|
| Free audit / diagnosis (type 1) | B2B and professional services ("auditoría de [área] en 30 min"), home services (inspection), health (assessment) |
| Calculator or quiz (type 1 or 3) | Real estate (mortgage/credit capacity), solar (savings), education (level test). Reading quizzes as "reveal their problem" magnets is my inference. |
| Trial / sample (type 2) | Gyms (trial week), education (trial class), coaching (a first working session) |
| Win-your-money-back | Fitness and education (refund or credit on attendance/completion). The conditions must be written down and verifiable. |
| Decoy / anchoring | Clinics and med-spas (basic package next to premium), agencies (basic diagnosis next to a full plan) |

**Copy skeletons built from the cited components (English, then MX):**
- **"Reveal the problem" audit ad** (Hormozi callout + type-1 magnet + Suby bold promise):
  - EN: "[Business type] in [City]: in a free 30-minute [audit], we'll show you exactly where you're losing [leads/money/time] — and the 3 fixes we'd make first. If we can't find at least [N] opportunities, you keep the action plan anyway. Tap 'Learn more' to book."
  - MX: "[Tipo de negocio] en [Ciudad]: en una auditoría gratuita de 30 min te mostramos dónde estás perdiendo [prospectos/dinero/tiempo] y las 3 correcciones que haríamos primero. Si no encontramos al menos [N] oportunidades, igual te quedas con el plan de acción. Toca 'Más información' para agendar."
- **Trial offer** (type 2 + time and effort reduction):
  - MX: "Clase muestra GRATIS de [curso] en [plantel/online] — sin inscripción y sin tarjeta. Sales con [resultado concreto de la clase]. Cupo por grupo: [N] (real)."
- **Win-your-money-back** (*Money Models*):
  - MX: "Reto [6] semanas en [gimnasio, colonia]: si asistes a [X] sesiones y no logras [meta medible], te devolvemos tu inscripción. Condiciones por escrito. Lugares para esta generación: [N]."
- **Decoy** (*Money Models*):
  - MX: "Valoración básica sin costo o Plan Completo [nombre] con [bonos]. Tú eliges."
- **Rules for urgency and scarcity:** keep them real (seasonal, capacity-based or cohort-based). This is my inference, supported by Haynes's view that a bad offer raises every cost downstream. Check PROFECO rules on truthful advertising (see Gaps).

- Mexican legal constraints on promotions, guarantees and sweepstakes (PROFECO, SEGOB) were not researched.

## 6. 2025-2026 performance data, CPL benchmarks, and how Andromeda / Advantage+ changed lead-gen tactics

### Takeaway
US Meta lead costs:
- **2025:** average CPL of **$27.66**, up about 21% YoY.
- **2026:** roughly flat at **$27.39**.
- Dental is the most expensive vertical ($76.71 -> $61.56), and real estate is among the cheapest ($16.61 -> $13.74).

Three platform shifts in 2025 moved lead-gen tactics away from audience and interest hacking, and from friction alone, toward **conceptually diverse creative plus CRM quality signals**:
- **Andromeda** retrieval, announced December 2024. Meta reports +6% recall and +8% ads quality.
- **Advantage+ leads.** Launched around February 2025 and available globally by October 2025, with website and instant forms in one campaign; Meta claims 14-24% lower CPL versus website forms alone.
- **CRM-based conversion-leads optimization.** Meta reports about 15% lower cost per quality lead.

### Cited Findings

#### Benchmarks
- **2025, WordStream/LocaliQ** (1,000+ campaigns):
  - leads objective average CPL **$27.66**, **+20.94% YoY**
  - CPL rose in 9 of 15 lead-campaign industries
  - "Industries with the highest cost per lead were Dentists and Dental Services ($76.71), Health and Fitness ($52.98), and Beauty and Personal Care ($51.42)."

  Source: [WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025).
- More 2025 context: lead-campaign CTR 2.59% (flat YoY); traffic CPC $0.70 (-6.7%); traffic CTR 1.71% (up from 1.57%); Google Ads average CPL $70.11 for comparison. Sources: [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/facebook-ad-costs-jump-beat-google-461690); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/facebook-ad-costs-jump-21-as-lead-campaigns-struggle-while-traffic-ads-thrive/).
- **2026, WordStream/LocaliQ:**
  - average CPL **$27.39** (vs $27.66)
  - lowest: Career & Employment $12.30, Real Estate $13.74, Arts & Entertainment $14.59
  - highest: Dentists $61.56, Beauty & Personal Care $50.91, Home & Home Improvement $42.95
  - "Most industries saw a decrease in average cost per lead, but Home and Home Improvement saw a CPL increase of 4.10% as well as Personal Services with a CPL increase of 24.60%."

  Sources: [WordStream 2026](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2026); [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/); [AdManage summary](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ads-cost-per-lead-benchmarks).
- **A different method** (Focus Digital: 138 North American campaigns, March-June 2026): Legal $72.40 and Financial Services $58.70 have the highest CPLs. Source: [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-cost-per-lead-on-facebook-july-2026-report/).
- **Data-labelling conflict:** Superscale lists a lead-campaign CPC of $1.92 labelled as 2026, but its own table cites October 2025 data. Compare numbers only within the same source and objective. Source: [Superscale](https://superscale.ai/learn/facebook-ads-cost/).

#### Andromeda
- Andromeda is the retrieval stage of ad delivery: it narrows tens of millions of eligible ads to a few thousand candidates, which later models rank. Meta announced it in December 2024 (dated 2 Dec 2024 by secondary sources), designed for NVIDIA Grace Hopper and MTIA hardware. Sources: [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/401591/meta-discloses-andromeda-ad-retrieval.html); [Selzee](https://selzee.com/what-is-meta-andromeda); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-next-gen-ad-strategies).
- Meta, as quoted by Social Media Today: "+6% recall improvement to the retrieval system, delivering +8% ads quality improvement on selected segments." Source: [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-shares-insight-into-the-performance-of-its-ai-based-ad-elements/734328/).
- Practitioner consensus:
  - Creative now does much of the audience matching ("creative relevance effectively becomes the audience").
  - Diversity must be conceptual (persona, problem, awareness level, angle, concept, format); changing the first sentence of a testimonial is only a variation of the same concept.
  - Volume guidance varies: at least 6 meaningfully different ads per ad set and 15-20 active, or 10-15 conceptually distinct assets per Advantage+ campaign.

  Sources: [Creative Brackets](https://creative-brackets.com/blog/meta-andromeda-why-creative-diversification-matters/); [Karb.ai](https://karb.ai/blog/meta-ads-creative-diversification-2026); [Chatterbuzz](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/); [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/metas-2026-algorithm-update-what-andromeda-changed-and-how-to-adapt-your-ads/); [Uproas](https://www.uproas.io/blog/meta-andromeda-update-explained) (vendor/agency blogs).
- A practitioner write-up (not peer-reviewed) reports that strategically unique ads give more stable costs and longer creative lifespans than high-volume dynamic creative. Source: [Totter, Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/144136338/Metas_Andromeda_Algorithm_and_Creative_Diversification_Best_Practice_Guide).

#### Advantage+ leads
- A Search Engine Land headline dated **2025-02-07** reads "Meta simplifies Advantage+ campaign setup, adds leads campaigns." The campaigns combine Advantage+ placements, Advantage+ audience and budget optimization. Early claims are about 9-10% lower cost per lead or action (Meta-reported). Sources: [Birch](https://bir.ch/blog/meta-marketing-updates); [IMM](https://imm.com/blog/unpacking-meta-2025-ad-overhaul-andromeda-advantage-and-what-it-means-for-your-ads); [Madgicx](https://madgicx.com/blog/new-advantage-updates); [Coinis](https://coinis.com/blog/meta-advantage-plus-ai-ads-updates-2025). These are secondary.
- Global availability, website and instant forms in one campaign, and the 14-24% lower CPL claim versus website forms alone (around October 2025). Source: [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-lead-gen-ad-updates-ai-targeting-verification-crm-expansion/804301/).
- A blog claims that from June 2025, Sales, Leads and App campaigns run in Advantage+ mode by default. This is **unverified**. Source: [IMM](https://imm.com/blog/unpacking-meta-2025-ad-overhaul-andromeda-advantage-and-what-it-means-for-your-ads) (or a similar blog in the same search results).

#### Lead quality and signals
- Meta reports conversion-leads optimization with CAPI for CRM gives about 15% lower cost per quality lead (other summaries: about 19%; SumUp case: 16%). Sources: [Click Here Digital](https://www.clickheredigital.com/insights/meta-shift-to-lead-optimization); [LeadsBridge](https://leadsbridge.com/blog/conversion-leads-optimization-facebook/).

#### How gurus' creative advice lines up with this
- Hormozi (2025): high creative volume, hook multiplication and a 70/20/10 split. Source: [Shortform Ep 878](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-05-03-episode-summary-brutally-honest-business-advice-ep-878).
- Haynes: no-CTA content ads alongside direct-response ads. Source: [jeremyhaynes.com](https://jeremyhaynes.com/skills/content-ads-cold-traffic/).
- Piliero (February 2025, DTC): "stop stacking interests. One interest per ad set." Source: [X](https://x.com/SamPiliero/status/1889380825063477669).

### Inferences
- **Benchmarks for Superleads:** use US CPLs only as a relative ranking of verticals, e.g., dental and aesthetics are expensive; real estate and legal are cheap on Meta per WordStream but expensive in Focus Digital's data. Do not use them as Mexican price targets; Mexican CPMs and CPLs differ and were not researched here. Judge clients on cost per *qualified* lead or appointment from the CRM, which is exactly the signal Meta's conversion-leads optimization uses.
- **What still works after Andromeda / Advantage+:**
  - Creative-level frameworks: Hormozi callout-value-CTA, Brunson hook-story-offer, Suby's HVCO/Godfather offer, Haynes content ads.
  - Funnel-level qualification: forms with conditional logic, VSL applications, CTWA flows.
- **What to adjust:**
  1. Turn each "template" into several conceptually distinct ads (different personas and callouts, pains, formats, messengers such as the owner, a customer or an expert), not cosmetic variations.
  2. Replace interest stacking and lookalike tinkering with broad or Advantage+ audiences plus creative that self-selects the right person.
  3. Fix quality with signals (CAPI for CRM, verification) instead of relying only on form friction.
  4. Hormozi's 70/20/10 maps well onto Andromeda if the "new" 10-20% is used for new concepts.
- **Possible tension:** Hormozi's "multiply one message by many hooks" can produce many near-duplicate ads, which vendors say Andromeda treats as one concept. Agencies should vary angle and persona, not just the opening line. This tension is my inference from the cited vendor guidance; it is not a documented Meta statement.

- Meta's own lead-gen case studies (Meta for Business success stories) were not retrieved (blocked).
- No Mexico/LatAm CPL benchmarks were researched; the WordStream/LocaliQ data is US.
- I could not open the full WordStream/LocaliQ tables (CTR, CVR, CPC by industry for 2026), and the 2026 Attorneys & Legal figure did not appear in the extracts.
- The exact Andromeda rollout timing (announced December 2024 vs. vendor claims of a "mid-2025" rollout) remains uncertain.
